## 🛡️ BB84 Demo - Quantum Cryptography (35 seconds)

### 1. Launch BB84 Mission (5 seconds)
- **Action**: Click MainMenu → Protocols → BB84
- **Narrate**: "Let's start with BB84 quantum key distribution — the protocol that protects quantum internet communications."

### 2. Fly Through Quantum World (15 seconds)
- **Action**: Use WASD+mouse to fly around the 3 pulsing quantum spheres
- **Narrate**: "I'm now flying my drone through a live quantum simulation. These spheres are actual qubits — their colors show quantum phases, sizes show measurement probabilities."

### 3. Demonstrate Eve Attack (15 seconds)
- **Action**: Press E key to toggle Eve eavesdropping
- **Narrate**: "Watch what happens when I activate Eve attack — the error rate spikes to orange and red. The quantum protocol detects the eavesdropping automatically!"
- **Point out**: Error rate changes from green to red, "Eve: DETECTED!" alert

---

## ⚔️ Grover Demo - Quantum Algorithm (35 seconds)

### 4. Switch to Grover Mission (5 seconds)
- **Action**: ESC → MainMenu → Algorithms → Grover's Search
- **Narrate**: "Now let's see a quantum algorithm attack — Grover's search through an encrypted database."

### 5. Show Database Grid (15 seconds)
- **Action**: Fly around the 2×4 grid of database items
- **Narrate**: "Here's our encrypted database — 8 items total. Classically, you'd need to search through 4 items on average. But watch what quantum computing does..."

### 6. Quantum vs Classical Advantage (15 seconds)
- **Action**: Watch probabilities rise, point to HUD showing success rate
- **Narrate**: "The red bar is our target — item 5. See how it rises above all others? Success rate jumps from 12.5% classical to 90% quantum. That's quadratic speedup!"

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Tips

### If Backend Connection Fails
- **Quick Fix**: Show backend terminal with green "Online" status
- **Backup Plan**: Use pre-recorded video or slides showing key features

### If Godot Lags
- **Alternative**: Focus on HUD updates and statistics rather than smooth flight

### If Time Runs Short
- **Essential**: BB84 Eve attack demonstration (most impressive visual)
- **Skip**: Detailed flight sequences, focus on quantum effects
